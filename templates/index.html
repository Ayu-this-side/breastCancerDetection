Breast Cancer Awareness & Early Detection

Early detection saves lives. Understand key screening practices and clinical indicators.

1

Regular Self-Examinations

Perform monthly breast self-exams to become familiar with natural tissue texture and detect unexpected lumps, skin changes, or tenderness early.

2

Clinical Mammography

Annual or biennial mammograms are recommended for women starting between ages 40 to 50, providing high-resolution imaging before physical symptoms appear.

3

Fine Needle Aspiration (FNA)

FNA biopsies extract cellular samples from suspicious masses. Cytological features like cell radius, texture, and concavity form the input vector analyzed by our model.
